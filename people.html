Prospective postdocs and students

LANL has a large postdoc community, and it’s a great place to work with mentors of widely-varied expertise. If you’re interested in a postdoc position working on phylogenetic models of viral evolution (or other kinds of math models of infectious disease), I’m happy to discuss opportunities here.

LANL also hosts graduate, undergraduate, and high school students for summer research internships. If you’re interested, early winter is the time to get in touch.
